Connect this app to your PPL Powerbuilding Tracker Google Sheet.
Four 4-week blocks. Heavy days (Mon Squat, Tue Bench, Wed Deadlift) use one RPE top set plus two backoffs at about 90% of the top set. RPE climbs 6 / 7 / 8 / 9 across each block and top-set reps drop 6 → 4 → 3 → 2. Fri (Upper) and Sat (Lower) are the higher-rep hypertrophy bench and squat days. Weights come from your training maxes in the Sheet's Plan tab: change the starting 1RMs there, then tap ☁ to pull the updated plan into the app.
